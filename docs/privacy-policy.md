# Yard Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

Yard is an independent community marketplace. This policy describes the data handled by the Yard iOS app and service. Replace `support@yard.market` and the public policy URL with verified operator-controlled contact details before release.

## Data we use

- Sign in with Apple supplies a stable account identifier and, only when Apple provides it, a name or relay email.
- A Harvard-managed email address is collected solely to confirm community eligibility. The six-digit verification code is short-lived and stored only as a keyed hash.
- Listings include text, item photos, price, condition, and a coarse public pickup area chosen by the seller.
- Reservations, waitlists, messages, pickup proposals, reports, blocks, and moderation records are stored to operate and protect the marketplace.
- Device push tokens are stored to deliver transactional notifications.
- Product events such as searches, listing views, saves, and successful reservations may be recorded to improve relevance and marketplace health. Yard does not use third-party advertising tracking.
- Approximate or precise device location may be used on-device, with permission, to calculate walking directions and ETA to a public pickup area. Yard does not send continuous or precise device location to its backend.

## Sharing and retention

Public listings show a display name and coarse pickup area, not a Harvard email, Apple identifier, room number, or precise address. Data is shared with infrastructure providers only to operate storage, email, notifications, moderation, and hosting. Yard does not sell personal information or share it for cross-app advertising.

Users can delete their account in Profile. Sign-in identity and Harvard email are removed and unsold listings are taken down. Transaction and safety records may be pseudonymized and retained for fraud prevention, dispute handling, legal obligations, and community safety. Operational backups expire on their configured schedule.

## Choices and contact

Camera, photo-library, location, and notification permissions are optional and controlled in iOS Settings, although item photos are required to publish. Users can request access, correction, or deletion through the in-app account controls or `support@yard.market`.

Yard is an independent community marketplace and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Harvard University.
