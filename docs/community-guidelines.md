# Yard Community Guidelines

- Describe every item honestly, including defects, condition, price, and what is included.
- Use your own item photos. Do not copy misleading images or impersonate another seller.
- Communicate respectfully and keep coordination in Yard so safety tooling can work.
- Meet in a well-lit public area. Never require a room number, residence address, or isolated meetup.
- Do not request passwords, verification codes, banking credentials, identity documents, or unnecessary personal data.
- Honor active reservations and agreed times. Cancel promptly when plans change.
- Inspect items before completing an exchange. Yard does not provide in-app payment or escrow.
- Report suspected fraud, threats, harassment, unsafe behavior, prohibited items, and material misrepresentation.

Moderators may remove content, warn or suspend accounts, and preserve pseudonymized safety records. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented, and reviewable by an authorized administrator.
