# Prohibited Items Policy

Yard prohibits weapons and weapon parts, ammunition, explosives, illegal or controlled drugs, prescription drugs, alcohol, nicotine products, stolen goods, counterfeit goods, explicit sexual content or services, hazardous materials, recalled products, fraudulent documents, live animals, and anything prohibited by applicable law or Apple policy.

Listings that evade these categories through euphemisms, bundles, photos, or private messages are also prohibited. Moderation is defense in depth: deterministic text rules operate in every environment, production images are reviewed through the configured moderation provider, users can report content or conduct, and administrators can remove listings or suspend accounts. A failed required production moderation service never silently publishes an item.
